## Guardrail

**This profile calibrates voice, never content.** No sentence, argument, distinctive phrase, or
example quoted anywhere in this document may be transplanted — verbatim or lightly paraphrased
— into future writing. This document exists to answer *how the writing should sound*; it must
never be used as a source of *what the writing should say*. Any future use of a corpus sample's
ideas, facts, or text requires separate sourcing/citation, never silent incorporation through
this profile.
